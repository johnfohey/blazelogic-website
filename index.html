Meet Blaze

Blaze was only a couple of years old when a car took her from us. But in that short time, she became an unforgettable part of her person's life — waiting at the door when he came home, following him everywhere, always close by.

When it came time to name a new company, there was really only one choice. Today, Blaze is the heart of BlazeLogic — her name is on the company, her face is part of our identity, and she rides along for every round.
