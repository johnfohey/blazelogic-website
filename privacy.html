Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

BlazeLogic LLC ("BlazeLogic", "we") runs blazelogic.io as a simple, informational website. This policy explains what information we collect and what we do with it — in plain language.

Information you send us

If you use the contact form, you share your name, email address, and message. That information is delivered to us through our form provider (Web3Forms) and lands in our inbox at john@blazelogic.io so we can read and reply to it. We keep messages as long as needed to respond, and we never sell or share your contact details with anyone else.

Information we don't collect

We don't have user accounts, we don't process payments on this site, and we don't run analytics or advertising trackers. The interactive Nassau demo runs entirely in your browser — nothing you tap there is sent to us.

Cookies

This site doesn't set its own cookies.

Links to other services

Our podcast player can take you to Spotify or Apple Podcasts, and the site links to Instagram. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave blazelogic.io.

Deleting your Golf Bets data

Golf Bets stores the players, rounds, scores, presses, settlements, history and stats you create, under an anonymous account that isn't linked to your email or phone number. You can delete all of it at any time:

  • In the app: on the Home screen tap the gear icon, then Privacy & Data → Delete My Data.
  • On the web: in the app, tap the gear icon, then Privacy & Data → Get Deletion Code, and enter the code at blazelogic.io/delete. A code works once and expires after 24 hours.
  • By email: if you can't use either option, email privacy@blazelogic.io. We complete requests we can verify within 30 days.

Deletion is permanent. When it finishes, your Golf Bets data and anonymous account are removed from our database. Our current database plan doesn't include backups, so deleted data isn't kept in a backup that could be restored. Deleting the app from your phone doesn't delete your data on our servers, so use one of the options above first.

Questions

For anything privacy-related, email privacy@blazelogic.io.
