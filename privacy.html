Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

BlazeLogic LLC ("BlazeLogic", "we") runs blazelogic.io as a simple, informational website. This policy explains what information we collect and what we do with it — in plain language.

Information you send us

If you use the contact form, you share your name, email address, and message. That information is delivered to us through our form provider (Web3Forms) and lands in our inbox at john@blazelogic.io so we can read and reply to it. We keep messages as long as needed to respond, and we never sell or share your contact details with anyone else.

Information we don't collect

We don't have user accounts, we don't process payments on this site, and we don't run analytics or advertising trackers. The interactive Nassau demo runs entirely in your browser — nothing you tap there is sent to us.

Cookies

This site doesn't set its own cookies.

Links to other services

Our podcast player can take you to Spotify or Apple Podcasts, and the site links to Instagram. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave blazelogic.io.

Questions

For anything privacy-related, email john@blazelogic.io.
