Privacy

Delete your Golf Bets data

The quickest way is in the app: on the Home screen tap the gear icon, then Privacy & Data → Delete My Data.

Can't use the app right now? In Golf Bets, tap the gear icon, then Privacy & Data → Get Deletion Code, and enter that code below. A code works once and expires 24 hours after you create it. Getting a new code cancels the old one.

This permanently deletes your players, rounds, scores, presses, settlements, history and stats. It can't be undone.

No code, or lost your phone?

Golf Bets uses an anonymous account that isn't linked to your email or phone number, so a Deletion Code from the app is how we confirm a request comes from you. If you can't get a code, email privacy@blazelogic.io and tell us what you can about your data (for example, player names and roughly when you played). We'll delete data we can verify within 30 days.

Deleting the app from your phone doesn't delete your data on our servers. Use the steps above first.

See our Privacy Policy for more.
